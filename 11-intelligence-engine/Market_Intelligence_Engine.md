# Market Intelligence Engine Specification
## Yemen Pharmaceutical Market Intelligence Platform

This document defines the core logic of the Intelligence Engine, which is the heart of the platform. It transforms raw data (Offers, Requests, including free-text entries) into actionable Market Signals, identifying shortages, surpluses, and hidden inventory with a calculated Confidence Score. The engine operates independently of any transaction processing, focusing solely on market visibility.

### 1. Signal Model (Market Signal)
A **Market Signal** is an automated insight generated by the Intelligence Engine. It is the primary output of the platform, replacing the traditional "CRUD" view of offers and requests.

**Signal Attributes:**
| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| `Signal ID` | Unique identifier for the signal. |
| `Signal Type` | Shortage, Surplus, Invisible Inventory, Price Anomaly, Trend Shift. |
| `Drug` | The specific drug associated with the signal (either an official ID or a free-text name). |
| `Location` | Region, Governorate, or City. |
| `Severity` | Low, Medium, High, Critical. |
| `Confidence` | A percentage (0-100%) indicating the reliability of the signal based on data volume and recency. |
| `Generated From` | The source data points (e.g., "Based on 5 active requests and 0 offers in Aden"). |
| `Created At` | Timestamp of signal generation. |
| `Status` | New, Acknowledged, Dismissed, Resolved. |

### 2. Core Detection Algorithms

#### 2.1. Shortage Detection (شح)
The engine detects a shortage when demand significantly outpaces supply in a specific geographic area.
- **Logic**: Calculate the `Supply-Demand Ratio` for a drug (official or free-text) in a Governorate over the last 7 days.
- **Formula**: `Requests_Weighted / Offers_Weighted`.
- **Trigger**: If the ratio exceeds a threshold (e.g., 2.0), a **Shortage Signal** is generated.
- **Confidence Score**: Increases with the number of distinct entities requesting the drug and the frequency of requests.

#### 2.2. Surplus Detection (فائض)
The engine detects a surplus when supply significantly exceeds demand.
- **Logic**: Calculate the `Supply-Demand Ratio` for a drug in a Governorate.
- **Trigger**: If the ratio is below a threshold (e.g., 0.5), a **Surplus Signal** is generated.
- **Confidence Score**: Increases with the volume of offers and their expiry dates.

#### 2.3. Invisible Inventory Discovery (المخزون غير المرئي)
The engine identifies entities that are active on the platform but not listing offers, potentially holding hidden stock.
- **Logic**: Identify verified entities in a Governorate experiencing a Shortage Signal for a specific drug, but the entity has no active offers for that drug.
- **Trigger**: If an entity's profile indicates they typically stock such drugs, a **Hidden Inventory Signal** is generated, prompting the platform to send a gentle nudge to the entity.

#### 2.4. Demand & Supply Indicators (مؤشرات الطلب والعرض)
The engine continuously calculates macro-level indicators.
- **Demand Index**: The total volume of requests for a drug category, weighted by urgency.
- **Supply Index**: The total volume of active offers, weighted by remaining shelf life (expiry date).

### 3. The Market Timeline (الزمن)
Every signal and metric must be contextualized within a time dimension.
- **History**: The engine maintains a daily snapshot of the Supply-Demand Ratio for every drug and region.
- **Trend**: By comparing the current ratio with the 7-day and 30-day moving averages, the engine classifies the market as:
  - `Growing` (Demand increasing)
  - `Declining` (Supply increasing)
  - `Stable`
  - `Volatile`

### 4. Notification and Alerting
When a high-severity or high-confidence signal is generated, the Notification Service is triggered.
- Alerts are sent to relevant entities (e.g., distributors are alerted to shortages; pharmacies are alerted to surpluses).
- Alerts include the Confidence Score to help users gauge the reliability of the intelligence.
