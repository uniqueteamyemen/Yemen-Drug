# Business Dictionary
## Yemen Pharmaceutical Market Intelligence Platform

This document defines the core terminology used within the Yemen Pharmaceutical Market Intelligence Platform. It serves as the single source of truth for business concepts, ensuring clarity across all product and engineering teams.

### Core Entities

| Term | Definition | Purpose | Relationships |
|------|------------|---------|---------------|
| **Entity** | A registered organization or individual using the platform (e.g., pharmacy, hospital, distributor). | To represent the actors who supply or demand pharmaceutical products. | Can create Offers and Requests. Located in a Region. |
| **Offer** | A listing indicating that an Entity has a surplus or available stock of a specific Drug. | To make available inventory visible to the market. | Belongs to an Entity. References a Drug. Can be matched with a Request. |
| **Request** | A listing indicating that an Entity needs to procure a specific Drug. | To express demand and trigger the matching process. | Belongs to an Entity. References a Drug. Can be matched with an Offer. |
| **Drug** | A specific pharmaceutical product identified by brand name, active ingredient, and manufacturer. | To standardize the products being traded on the platform. | Referenced by Offers and Requests. Has Alternatives. Based on the official list, but allows free text for unlisted items. |
| **Alternative** | A different Drug that can serve as a substitute for another Drug based on active ingredients or therapeutic use. | To help fulfill requests when the exact requested Drug is unavailable. | Related to one or more Drugs. |

### Market Intelligence Concepts

| Term | Definition | Purpose | Relationships |
|------|------------|---------|---------------|
| **Inventory** | The known, active stock reported by an Entity via Offers. | To track available supply in the market. | Aggregated from Offers. |
| **Invisible Inventory** | Stock that exists within Entities but is not currently listed as an Offer (e.g., safety stock, near-expiry items). | To uncover hidden supply to prevent artificial shortages. | Identified through Market Intelligence analysis. |
| **Shortage** | A state where demand (Requests) for a Drug significantly exceeds the available supply (Offers) in a specific Region. | To alert stakeholders of critical supply gaps. | Identified by comparing Requests and Offers per Region. |
| **Surplus** | A state where supply (Offers) for a Drug significantly exceeds the demand (Requests) in a specific Region. | To alert stakeholders of potential waste or over-supply. | Identified by comparing Offers and Requests per Region. |
| **Market Signal** | An automated insight or alert generated by the Intelligence Engine based on market data (e.g., rising demand, detected shortage). | To provide actionable intelligence to users and analysts. | Triggered by the Intelligence Engine. Carries a Confidence Score. |
| **Confidence Score** | A percentage (0-100%) indicating the reliability of a Market Signal or Match based on data volume and recency. | To help users gauge the accuracy of the intelligence provided. | Attribute of a Market Signal or Match. |

### System & Interaction Terms

| Term | Definition | Purpose | Relationships |
|------|------------|---------|---------------|
| **Event** | A significant change in the state of an entity or a market condition (e.g., OfferCreated, ShortageDetected). | To drive asynchronous processing and trigger system responses. | Triggers Notifications or Signals. |
| **Notification** | A message sent to an Entity to inform them of a relevant Event or Signal (e.g., "New match for your request"). | To engage users and prompt action. | Associated with an Entity. |
| **Conversation** | A communication thread between two Entities, typically initiated after a Match. | To allow negotiation and coordination, leading to the exchange of contact information. | Involves two Entities. |

### Geographic & Attribute Terms

| Term | Definition | Purpose | Relationships |
|------|------------|---------|---------------|
| **Region** | A broad geographic area used for market segmentation (e.g., North Yemen, South Yemen). | To group Cities and Governorates for macro-level analysis. | Contains Governorates. |
| **Governorate** | An administrative division within a Region. | To track supply and demand at a provincial level. | Belongs to a Region. Contains Cities. |
| **City** | A specific urban area where Entities operate. | To pinpoint exact locations for logistics and matching. | Belongs to a Governorate. |
| **Urgency** | A classification of how critical a Request is (e.g., Low, Medium, High, Critical). | To prioritize matching and notifications. | Attribute of a Request. |
| **Status** | The current state of an Entity's lifecycle object (e.g., Draft, Active, Matched, Closed, Expired). | To track the progress and validity of Offers and Requests. | Attribute of Offers, Requests, and Entities. |
